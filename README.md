# granviaboltnew

Granvia supports employer company management by its own staff: Super Admin creates department roles and employees, allocates company departments to them, and staff manage their allocated work through **My Company Departments**. Supported departments are HR, Sales, Operations and Accounts. See the [functionality, setup and verification guide](docs/GRANVIA_MANAGED_COMPANY_DEPARTMENTS.md).
