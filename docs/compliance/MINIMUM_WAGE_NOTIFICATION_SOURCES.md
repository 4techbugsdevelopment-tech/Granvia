# Minimum Wage Notification Sources

Only legally authoritative sources may support an approved rate:

- Ministry of Labour and Employment
- Chief Labour Commissioner (Central)
- State Labour Departments
- Union Territory administrations
- official government gazettes
- another official repository verified by the compliance owner

Each imported record must retain its notification number, issuing authority, publication/effective dates, official URL or stored document reference, and optionally a document hash. AI extraction, web scraping, spreadsheets, or manual transcription may help prepare a draft, but none of them is approval evidence by itself.

No government wage rates were added by this implementation. Compliance owners must source and approve actual notifications before active job publishing can pass.
